# introduction

<Faces names={['ada@lovelace.dev', 'agent-47', 'soggy-glorp', 'grace@hopper.dev', 'bot-9000']} size={72} />

glorpies paints an ugly little alien for any string: antennae, extra eyes, horns, halos and too many teeth, in watercolour and dip pen. Give it a username, an email or a bot's id, and it paints that name's face, the same one every time, on every device and every server. The faces are alive, too: they breathe, blink and glance at you.

## why

Most apps show a grey silhouette or two initials until someone uploads a photo. glorpies gives every user, bot and agent a face of its own, from the id you already have.

- **Nothing to store.** A name hashes to one of 9,007,199,254,740,992 seeds, and the seed decides everything. There's no database and no API to call.
- **Every framework.** Components for React, Vue, Svelte, Solid and Preact; a web component for Angular, Astro and plain HTML; image files from a server route; a CLI.
- **Small and dependency-free.** Plain TypeScript with no runtime dependencies. Faces paint lazily and share one animation loop.
- **Moods for agents.** Six states put an agent's status on its face: thinking, working, waiting, blocked, done.

## how it works

The name becomes a 53-bit seed, and the seed drives a random stream that builds the alien as a list of drawing operations: glazes, hatching, pen lines and fills. The list is painted in the look you pick, and the eyes are drawn live on top. [Watch one being painted](/how).
