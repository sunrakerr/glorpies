# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- 9,007,199,254,740,992 faces: any string gets its own, the same one every time, everywhere.
- 29 looks: 21 paintings for faces shown large, 8 avatar looks that read at 16px.
- Alive: breathing, blinking and glancing, with six states for agents (`idle`, `thinking`, `working`, `waiting`, `blocked`, `done`).
- `background={false}` for only the face, in every look.
- A `<Glorpie>` component for React, Vue, Svelte, Solid and Preact, each rendering a placeholder on the server.
- The `<glorpie-face>` element for Angular, Astro and plain HTML (also from a CDN), `mount()` for your own canvas, and `faceSVG()`.
- `glorpies/server`: PNG, WebP, JPEG, SVG and looping GIFs from a route in your own app, with `faceRoute()`, `createFaceHandler()` and `nodeFaceHandler()`.
- The `glorpies` CLI.
